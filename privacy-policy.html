Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026 · App: Property Finance (Android) · Developer: SABBiR1107
In short: everything you enter in Property Finance stays on your device. We run no server, need no account, and never receive your property, tenant or money records. The app is free and shows ads from Google AdMob, which never receive those records either (see section 6).

1. About this policy

This policy explains how the Property Finance mobile app ("the app", "we") handles information. The app helps landlords record properties, units, tenants, leases, rent, bills, expenses and reports.

2. Information you enter stays on your device

Everything you enter — properties, units, tenant names and optional phone numbers or emails, leases, payments, bills, expenses, notes and settings — is stored in a private database inside the app on your device.

  • There is no account and no sign-in.
  • We do not operate any server for the app and cannot see, access, or recover your records.
  • Your records are not sent to us, sold, or shared with any third party.
  • Only enter the tenant details you need. You are responsible for having a lawful reason to keep your tenants' information.

3. Files you export or share

You can create backups (JSON files), reports (CSV files), tenant ledgers and rent receipts. These are created only when you ask, and saved or shared to a place you choose through your device's own file picker or share sheet. Backups are not encrypted, so keep them private. Tenant ledgers and receipts contain personal data; share them only with people entitled to see them.

4. Notifications

If you allow notifications, reminders (rent due, overdue rent, bills due, recurring expenses, leases ending) are scheduled locally on your device. No reminder content leaves your device. You can turn each reminder type off in Settings, and the app works fully if you deny notification permission.

5. App update alerts

If you turn on "Notify me when an update is available", the app asks the Google Play Store app on your device whether a newer version exists. The app does not send any of your records for this. Google Play's handling of your device and account is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

6. Advertising

The app is free and shows ads supplied by Google AdMob.

  • To show, limit and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address and approximate location, and your interactions with ads.
  • Your property, tenant and money records are never shared with Google or any advertiser, and ads are never targeted using anything you enter in the app.
  • Where required by law (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, and certain US states) the app asks for your consent, or lets you opt out, using Google's own consent tool. You can change that choice at any time in More → Privacy & about → Privacy & ad choices.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.
  • Ads never appear during first-time setup, in Settings, in Backup & restore, or on any screen where you enter or edit records.
  • More information: How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

7. Device permissions

  • Notifications — only to show the reminders and update alerts you turn on.
  • Run at startup — to restore your scheduled reminders after the phone restarts.
  • Internet and advertising ID — added by the Google Mobile Ads SDK, and used only to load and measure ads and to ask Google Play whether an app update exists. Your records are never uploaded.

The app does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone or photos. Files are opened and saved only through the system file picker when you choose to.

8. Deleting your data

In the app, open More → General → Delete all data to permanently remove every record from your device. Uninstalling the app also deletes its data. Because we never hold a copy, we cannot restore deleted data — keep your own backups. There is no account to delete.

9. Security

Your records are kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. The app does not add its own encryption, so protect your device with a screen lock and keep exported files safe.

10. Children

The app is intended for adults managing property and is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect information from children.

11. Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above.
