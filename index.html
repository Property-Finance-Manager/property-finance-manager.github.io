Bookkeeping that thinks like an accountant

Built around the questions landlords actually ask: who paid, who still owes, what did each property cost, and what's left.

Rent & partial payments

Rent due dates are created from each lease. Record full, partial or advance payments and choose which months they settle.

Arrears at a glance

See rent still owed by tenant and period, with days overdue, waivers and tenant credit kept separate.

Bills vs expenses

Unpaid bills stay in "bills to pay" until you pay them. Repairs, utilities and service charges, with recurring bills created automatically.

Clear reports

Cash-basis profit & loss, expense breakdown, property comparison and monthly trends. Export to CSV for your accountant.

Many properties

Apartments, houses, shops and whole buildings with units, tenants and leases. View the full portfolio or one property.

Backup & restore

Save a full backup to a place you choose and restore it on a new phone. Every correction keeps a visible history.

Honest numbers, kept separate

Mixing these up is how spreadsheets go wrong. Property Finance keeps them apart for you.

  • Rent owed vs rent receivedMoney is counted when it arrives; what's still due is shown on its own.
  • Unpaid bills vs paid expensesA bill becomes an expense only when you pay it.
  • Deposits are not incomeSecurity deposits, owner money and renovations never inflate your result.
  • Currencies never mixedProperties in different currencies are reported separately.

Reminders that help

Optional notifications for rent due soon, overdue rent, bills due and leases ending — scheduled on your phone.

Plus optional alerts when a new version of the app is available on Google Play.

Made for landlords everywhere

25 languages with local numbers and dates, right-to-left support, and every world currency.

Your records stay on your phone

No account and no server. Properties, tenants and payments are stored in a private database on your device and are never sent to us or to advertisers. You decide when to export or share a file.

The app is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. Ads appear only inside lists and reports — never on a form, in Settings, or over a button — and they are never targeted using anything you enter.
